Clear accidental imports

Removes posts that have no completed entry or permanent download, including failed posts, and their queued jobs. Partial extracted text and attachments are kept for undo. Pause processing and wait for the active post to finish first.

This changes your Unscroll library only. Your TikTok saves stay as they are. After removing, import a few links, then use Process selected or Process next 20.

Your API key stays in the local .env file. Only extracted post text, entry text and topic labels are sent to Claude. Models are downloaded once for local speech and search. Browser cookies are never read automatically.

Connect to the helper

The library lives in Chrome. The local helper handles media, speech and storage on your Mac.

Start the helper with Start.command, then paste the pairing token printed during setup. You can also find it in the helper’s local Settings.

This token pairs the extension with this Mac. Your Claude API key stays in the helper’s local configuration.
